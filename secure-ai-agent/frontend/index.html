Face login

Lets you sign in with your face. It runs in your browser, so a determined person with a good photo of you could get past it. Leave it off if your chats are sensitive. Your password always works, and is always needed for sensitive actions.

Delete account

Permanently deletes your chats, files, face data and security photos. This can't be undone.

Set up face login

Aperture stores a short list of numbers that describes your face (not your photo) and uses it only to recognise you at sign-in. If someone fails to sign in as you, a photo of them is saved to your Security activity. You can turn this off or delete everything at any time.

Security activity

Photos saved when someone failed to sign in as you with a face, or a different face appeared while monitoring. Only you can see these.
